What TriApp does

TriApp replaces static spreadsheets and fragmented trackers with a single, intelligent coach. It builds a personalized triathlon training plan based on your profile, race date, and past performance — and continuously adapts it as your circumstances change.

Garmin Connect integration

With your permission, TriApp connects to your Garmin Connect account to import completed workouts and daily health metrics (such as training load, heart rate, and sleep). This allows the platform to compare planned versus actual training and adjust upcoming sessions to keep you progressing safely.

Key features

  • AI-generated, multi-week triathlon training plans
  • Automatic adaptation based on completed workouts and fatigue
  • Detailed session instructions for swim, bike, run, and brick workouts
  • Calendar-aware scheduling around your day-to-day commitments
